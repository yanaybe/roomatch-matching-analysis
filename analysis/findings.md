# Findings (synthetic data)

_Generated by `analysis/run_analysis.py` from `results.json`._

## Q1: Does the score predict who connects?
- Mutual-like rate rises steadily with the score: 0.4% at the 15% floor → 12.1% at 75%+. AUC for a like: 0.648.
- But it is not a probability: pairs shown as "60-74% match" became mutual 7.8% of the time.
- 15.0% of all cards shown sat at the 15% floor. The code comment says these are suppressed, but nothing filters them.

## Q2: Which factors actually drive likes?
- Model on 325,054 swipes; tested on users the model never saw (95,118 swipes).
- Gender preference not met: **-18.6 pp** like probability. It is the largest effect, yet weighted zero in the score and not filtered. 23.1% of cards shown break the viewer's stated preference.
- Under-weighted by the app: budgets 50% apart -7.1 pp (index 67 vs app 20); cleanliness gap of 3 -8.0 pp (index 75 vs app 40); early bird vs. night owl -7.9 pp (index 74 vs app 51); move-in 4 months apart -7.9 pp (index 75 vs app 46)
- About right by the app: smoker vs. minds smoking -10.6 pp (index 100 vs app 100); religious gap of 2 -5.9 pp (index 55 vs app 51); pets vs. minds pets -5.0 pp (index 47 vs app 57)
- Over-weighted by the app: different lifestyle vibe +0.0 pp (index 0 vs app 34); social level 2 apart -1.3 pp (index 12 vs app 26)
- AUC on held-out users: score alone 0.646 → re-weighted app factors 0.719 → plus gender preference 0.790.

## Q3: Are good matches getting lost?
- 78.2% of likes go to someone who never sees the liker, so they can never become a match.
- When the target does see the liker, they like back 23.3% of the time.
- 71.7% of a profile's impressions happen in its first week.
- 23,281 of those unseen likes went to someone who opened the app again later. Showing them a "they liked you" queue is expected to add about 5,094 mutual likes on top of 1,321 today (model estimate, to be tested).

## Q4: Is the market liquid everywhere?
- Tel Aviv, the largest pool, gets 24.0% of new users a mutual like within 30 days, against 37.9% in Petah Tikva. Correlation between pool size and match rate: -0.41.
